🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you are a professional

  1. Cancer in one paragraph: cancer is not one disease but 100+ diseases where cells ignore stop signals, grow, recruit blood vessels, and spread. Staging (TNM) + grading + performance status decide treatment.
  2. Prevention → detection → treatment → survivorship: healthy habits + vaccines; screening (breast, cervical, colorectal, lung, prostate, skin); surgery/radiation/chemo/precision/immune/hormone/transplant/trials; then lifelong symptom + late-effects care.
  3. Symptoms travel in packs: fatigue, pain, poor sleep, low mood rise together. If you see one, screen for all four.
  4. Trials in one paragraph: phases 0–IV, randomization, consent, IRB, benefit vs risk. Modern trials add biomarkers + home-reported symptoms.
  5. Survivorship in one paragraph: after treatment ask: recurrence? new cancer? late effects (heart, bone, fertility, mood, money, work)? Keep a care plan and a primary-care partner for life.
  6. Do this now: run the Quick start, open results/live_snapshot.json and results/hidden_patterns.json, change one number, re-run. You now think like a researcher.

You will know more than most interview candidates once you can explain TNM, absolute vs relative risk, why fatigue is sentinel, and what NCCN Survivorship v3.2026 added.
